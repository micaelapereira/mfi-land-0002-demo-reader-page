Pulse — Reader Page: layout variants

Click-through demo for MadinMag (EWAG). A visitor sees the MadinMag ad on a venue's splash page in the airZoon network, opens the landing, reads the magazine in the page, and leaves a first name plus mobile or email to get new issues and event invitations.

Option A and option B differ only in the order of the page. A puts the magazine first and asks for the sign-up after it: reading comes first, and the visitor signs up once they like it. B puts the sign-up first and the magazine below it: on a phone the reader fills the screen and captures the scroll, so with A many visitors never reach the form. Content, copy and the three screens (empty form, filled form, confirmation) are the same in both.

Form fields: two versions to compare. "Mobile or email" asks for a first name plus one field for a mobile or an email: the shortest form. "Mobile + email" splits it into two fields so the visitor can leave both, which gives more contact data. In both, the first name is required, plus at least one of mobile or email. Switch between them with the gear icon.

About the gear icon (top left of each page): it is a review tool only and will not be part of the final page. It opens a bar to try the options in this demo, such as the screen, background, header or form fields.
